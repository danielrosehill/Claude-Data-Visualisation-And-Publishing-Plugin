Walk the user through the `choose-tool` decision protocol and recommend a library from the inventory.

1. Load `skills/choose-tool/SKILL.md` and `skills/tool-inventory/SKILL.md`.
2. Ask (or infer from `$ARGUMENTS`) the presentation purpose, data shape, runtime, interactivity need, and audience.
3. Recommend one inventory tool (or two with a short tradeoff) and state the reason in one sentence.
4. Offer to proceed to `/dataviz-setup` to scaffold the environment.

User task: $ARGUMENTS
